Locked spec — what every flow does

  1. Assign — click "Assign ▾" on any left-rail card, pick an operator. The card moves to the table for real, and lands at the bottom of that operator's queue (#N = last).
  2. Re-assign — click any table row to open the panel, click "Re-assign operator", pick someone else. The job drops to the bottom of the new operator's queue.
  3. Bulk re-assign — hover rows, tick 2+ (only Assigned/In-Progress rows can be ticked — Paused and Done are locked, shown with a grey lock, matching the real rule that a paused job stays with its operator until resume/done, and a done job can't be reassigned at all), pick an operator in the black bar, hit Reassign.
  4. Grouped by operator (v9 fix) — earlier the operator header only wrapped the waiting queue, so a running/paused/done job could land right after someone else's header with nothing to say it wasn't theirs (Chandrasing's in-progress job looked like part of Deepak's block). Fixed properly: each operator now owns ONE section holding everything of theirs, in this order top to bottom — what they're doing now (In Progress), what's next (their Seq 1, 2... queue), what's stuck (Paused), then history (Done). Still one flat table, not a filter/tab — just an honest grouping.
  5. Re-sequence — Seq is its own column, first, ahead of OBD (SO No. stays right after OBD, unchanged). It only applies to the waiting queue — other rows show a plain dash. Hover an Assigned row to reveal ▲▼ arrows in the Seq column — they swap the job one step within that SAME operator's queue only, matching the real reorder rule exactly (no cross-operator moves, one step at a time). If a job is already first/last in its operator's queue, that arrow is greyed out and does nothing. Vora Paints/Om Sai Traders (both Chandrasing) demonstrate a working swap; Bhagwati (Deepak, alone in his queue) demonstrates the disabled state.
  6. Remove OBD — only offered on a PENDING card (rail or its panel) — matches the real rule that removal is blocked once a job is assigned.
  7. Prev / Next in the panel walks the whole queue, rail cards first then table rows, without closing.
  8. Activity tab in the panel builds up as you act on a job — assign it, reassign it, and watch the log grow.
  9. Left on its own: after ~5s the In Progress job auto-finishes; after ~12s a new OBD arrives on the rail by itself — both simulate the live 15-30s refresh, not a page reload.
  10. Scope decision: the old "Create Split" flow (splitting one OBD across multiple operators) is DROPPED from this screen for now, on purpose — no fallback link. Only plain single-operator Assign ships in this build. Split-creation can come back as its own separate piece of work later if needed.
