v5 — what changed

Form

  • Two tabs at the top of the form: Cancel | Raise CI. Each tab shows only its own reasons and remarks.
  • Cancel tab — all ticked bills, cancel reasons, remarks. Red button "Cancel 3 bills".
  • Raise CI tab — all ticked bills. A bill whose invoice number hasn't reached Orbit yet gets a soft tag only — the CI still raises and the number appears when SAP sends it (CI module rule). CI reasons, remarks for billing. Brand button "Raise 3 CIs".
  • Opens on the Cancel tab by default; switching tabs keeps what was typed on the other tab.

Cancel & CI tab — column order

  • Action first (Cancel / CI), then the bill: ☐ · Action · OBD (+date) · Invoice · Ship to · Area · Vol / KG.
  • Then Reason · Remarks. No divider lines.
  • Then follow-up: CI no. (with billing / closed) · By · when — last, like Status is last on the Floor table.
  • Checkbox only on Cancel rows (only they can Restore). Dropped Due and Article — not useful once a bill is off the floor.

Still open

  • Cancel & CI tab: today only (as Cancelled is now), or a date stepper?
  • Cancel reason list and remarks need new storage — discovery checks this.
