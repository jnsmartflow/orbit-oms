This pass

  • One panel. The Pending / Closed tabs are gone — both live in the same rail under their own headings, the way Mail Orders keeps pending and done together. Closing a CI moves the card down the list in front of him instead of making it vanish into another tab.
  • Rail cards carry two things only — the CI number and the customer name. No return type, no litres, no value, in either section. The rail is for finding the CI; everything about it lives in the pane.
  • Closed cards are told apart by weight, not by data — grey ground and a lighter name, under their own heading.
  • The block is now "CI details", and its middle field is just CI number.
  • Header facts resequenced — invoice date → invoice no → received on → material → returned. Bill first, then the return, then the total.
  • CI details fields sequenced to match — CI date → CI number → value.
  • Row 2 of the header now carries the counts (4 pending · 13 closed) where the tabs used to be, with Filter and the date stepper on the right.

🔴 Two numbers still, even though only one is labelled

  • The field is now called plain CI number — that is 85832091, the one SAP gives back after punching.
  • CI-2026-00042 is a separate thing: OrbitOMS's own reference, given the moment the supervisor submits, shown top-right of the pane and on every rail card. It has to exist because a CI is real and needs referring to for hours before SAP ever sees it.
  • Your paper form carries both — 73989 at the top is the book serial, CI Order No 85832091 is SAP's. They stay two database columns, whatever the screen calls them; merging them would leave every pending CI unidentifiable.

New since the spec

  • Value (₹) — the 57,915/- circled at the bottom of your form. Needs a new decimal column.

Questions

  • If billing spots a mistake, nothing here lets them send it back. Should there be a Return to floor action?
  • Your paper form also has CI Punch by, Delivery No. and a separate Final CI No. — dead, or should Delivery No. be kept?
  • Does a closed CI need an A4 print sheet to replace the paper form?
