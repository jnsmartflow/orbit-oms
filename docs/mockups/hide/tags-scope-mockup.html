Mockup only — static HTML, nothing is wired. Admin-only: Settings › Hide › Tags (components/admin/hide-settings-content.tsx).
Option A “Who sees it”. One dropdown per tag — Everyone · Nobody · Everyone except… · Only… — with exceptions as chips beneath it and one plain sentence under those. Changing mode clears the previous mode’s exceptions, and the screen asks first.
Phase 1 is badges and bands. Hold / Slot / Urgent / the ship-to pencil are not here — they are capabilities, not badges, and belong to the per-user access ticks in phase 2.
Picker population: People are the active users who can open Mail Orders today, read from the live access source; Roles are the roles those people hold. Stored by role slug and user id.
Resolution: person > role > everyone. For a multi-role person, show wins — a role exception bites only if every role they hold hides it. No admin bypass.
