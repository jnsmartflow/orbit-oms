What differs from the Billing edit view

  • Gone, not greyed: the Paste SO column and Link on Not billed; the Unlink × column on Waiting for OBD. Each table drops that column and its width goes to the others — no empty track. A small “view only — Billing links SOs” chip replaces the missing action so nobody hunts for it.
  • Same: the four inner tabs, their counts, every row, the line-match view and History’s filter + search.
  • Floor mount: a fifth tab after “Cancel & CI”, same tabPill look and count chip (Not billed). While it is open the trip rail and bottom bar step aside and the screen takes the full width. The red double-dispatch banner shows here — Floor is who has to stop the load (D11).
  • Place Order mount: “My challan orders” sits left of “? shortcuts” in the top bar. It swaps the work area for the screen with “← Back to order”; the cart is kept. The list is the same as everywhere else (D8 “same lists”) — “My” is the link’s wording, not a filter.
