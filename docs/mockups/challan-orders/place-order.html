What changes, what does not (rev 1 — owner decisions S3-1…S3-7, 7 Oct)

  • New: the Challan order switch (tick holders only) · in challan mode: no Bill bar, two-mode Ship to, no Call, “Create challan order” in place of Send Email · the confirm dialog · the toast.
  • Switch OFF is today's screen, untouched — Bill tabs, the single ship-to box (still gated by place_order_ship_to), Normal / Urgent / Call, Send Email.
  • Switching ON with 2+ bills is refused (state 2) and the switch stays OFF. A single bill switches straight in, lines kept.
  • One brand button per state (CLAUDE_UI §10): Send Email when OFF, Create challan order when ON; an empty cart greys it, never fades it. Confirm button is gray-900 (§13).
  • Bill to is the customer chosen at the top (master only — F1a). Material codes come silently from the grid's own pack (F2). Base / tintable products go as plain goods (S3-5).
  • After create the cart resets (S3-7, state 6). The switch stays ON so the next challan can be keyed at once.
  • Not here: typed site address + contact — PARKED to ROADMAP (S3-3).
