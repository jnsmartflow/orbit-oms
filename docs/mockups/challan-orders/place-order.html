What changes, what does not

  • New: “Create challan order” (secondary, 34px, above Send Email) · the three-way Ship to · the confirm dialog · the toast.
  • Unchanged: top bar, customer pill, bill bar, line list, Dispatch, Order remarks, Notes, Total line, Send Email (still the only brand-600 button).
  • Confirm button is gray-900, not brand — the modal rule (CLAUDE_UI §13) and what send-confirm-overlay.tsx ships.
  • Bill to is the customer chosen at the top (master only, never typed — F1a). Material codes are resolved silently from the existing SKU mapping (F2) — no popup, nothing to show here.
  • After create, the active bill clears (shown as state 5). Whether ALL bills clear or only the one sent is open — see the reply notes.
