Challan orders — mockups

Phase 1, non-tint. The depot sends goods on a challan before SAP can bill; the Orbit number ORB-2026-00001 is the challan. Billing later pastes the SAP SO against it, and the SAP OBD that follows is linked, hidden from dispatch and matched line by line. Sample data throughout: MOHAN COLOUR CO (102425), GLOSS · BLACK 500ML × 36.
