What happened

    Rules

    • Two trucks to start; + Add truck for more. The last truck always gets the rest.
    • Whole tins only. Every truck needs at least 1 tin.
    • Only a part that is not dispatched can be split, held, or moved.
    • Splitting a held part → all new pieces come out held.
    • Splitting a part that is on a trip → the first piece stays on that trip, the rest wait.
    • A held part cannot be added to a trip.
    • Two parts of one invoice cannot sit on the same trip.
    • Bill is done when every part is dispatched.
    • Kg per part = SAP line kg ÷ line tins × tins. Exact.
